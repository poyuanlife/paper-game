紙片人遊樂園 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日

一句話說明：我們不蒐集、不上傳、不分享任何資料。照片和遊戲紀錄只存在你自己的裝置上。

適合的玩家

「紙片人遊樂園」（以下稱「本 App」）適合兒童和大人一起玩。我們依照兒童隱私相關規定設計本 App，包括美國《兒童線上隱私保護法》（COPPA）和 Google Play 家庭政策。

我們不蒐集的資料

  • 不需要註冊帳號，不會要你輸入姓名、電子郵件、電話或地址。
  • 沒有廣告，沒有內購。
  • 沒有分析或追蹤工具，不讀取廣告 ID、裝置識別碼或位置資料。
  • 本 App 不會連到外部網站或伺服器傳送資料。

相機與照片

  • 只有在你按「拍照」時才會開啟相機，也可以改從相簿選照片。
  • 去背、找身體關節都在你的裝置上完成（使用 Google MediaPipe 的裝置端模型），照片不會上傳。我們已關閉 MediaPipe 內建的使用統計回傳功能。
  • 只會儲存剪好的紙片人圖片和關節位置，存在你的裝置裡；原始照片不會另外保存。

動作感應器

部分「體感」遊戲會讀取手機的傾斜和搖晃，只用來操作遊戲，不會記錄或傳送。

分享圖片

結束遊戲後可以把成績做成圖片分享。分享前需要大人回答問題（家長關卡）；分享是透過手機內建的分享功能，由你自己選擇要分享給誰。我們不會收到這張圖片。

存在裝置上的資料

紙片人圖片、最高分、音效和語言設定等，都只存在你的裝置上。

怎麼刪除

  • 在「做紙片人」畫面按「🗑️ 刪除拍過的紙片人」，就會刪掉存著的紙片人圖片。
  • 解除安裝本 App（或清除網站資料），所有資料都會一起刪除。

政策變更

如果本政策有變更，會更新這一頁並修改上面的日期。

聯絡我們

有任何問題，請到 GitHub 專案頁 github.com/poyuanlife/paper-game 的 Issues 留言。

Paper Pals Playground Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: we do not collect, upload, or share any data. Photos and game records stay on your own device.

Who this app is for

Paper Pals Playground ("the App") is made for children and adults to play together. It is designed to follow children's privacy rules, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

Data we do not collect

  • No account is needed. We never ask for a name, email, phone number, or address.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking tools. We do not read advertising IDs, device identifiers, or location.
  • The App does not send data to any outside website or server.

Camera and photos

  • The camera opens only when you tap "Take Photo". You can also pick a photo from your gallery.
  • Background removal and body-joint detection run on your device (Google MediaPipe on-device models). Photos are never uploaded. We have turned off MediaPipe's built-in usage-statistics reporting.
  • Only the cut-out paper doll image and its joint positions are saved, on your device. The original photo is not kept.

Motion sensors

Some motion games read how the phone is tilted or shaken. This is used only to control the game and is never recorded or sent.

Sharing pictures

After a game you can turn your score into a picture and share it. A grown-up must answer a question first (parental gate). Sharing uses your phone's built-in share feature, and you choose who to share with. We never receive the picture.

Data stored on your device

Paper doll images, high scores, and sound and language settings are stored only on your device.

How to delete

  • On the "Make My Paper Doll" screen, tap "🗑️ Delete saved paper dolls" to remove the saved images.
  • Uninstalling the App (or clearing site data) removes all of its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

For any questions, please open an issue on the GitHub project page: github.com/poyuanlife/paper-game
